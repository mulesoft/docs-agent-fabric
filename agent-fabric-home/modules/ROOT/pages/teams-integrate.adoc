= Integrate the Enhanced MuleSoft Experience with Microsoft Teams
:keywords: microsoft teams integration, mulesoft agent, teams bot, mcp server, anypoint platform, enhanced experience

Get API Manager alert notifications directly in Teams channels through the MuleSoft for Teams app, and use the MuleSoft plugin in Microsoft Copilot to ask natural language questions and take action through Copilot Chat and Copilot Cowork. The plugin provides full capabilities of the xref:mulesoft-mcp-server::reference-mcp-tools-platform.adoc[MuleSoft Platform MCP Server].

* <<connect-teams-tenant, App setup>>: A Microsoft admin submits the app for review from the enhanced MuleSoft experience, approves the app in Microsoft Teams Admin Center, and binds your Microsoft Teams tenant to your Anypoint organization so the app can send alert notifications directly to channels and direct messages (DMs). A Microsoft Teams user chooses Teams channels that receive alert notifications in Teams.
* <<mulesoft-plugin, Plugin setup>>: A Microsoft admin installs the MuleSoft plugin through the Microsoft plugin registry to make the plugin available to Microsoft Copilot Chat and Copilot Cowork. Copilot is available from Microsoft Teams and other Microsoft product.

[[approve-app]]
=== (3) Approve and Publish the App in Microsoft Teams Admin Center

After the app is submitted for review, a Microsoft admin must publish it so users in your organization can add it.

. Log in to Microsoft Teams Admin Center.
. Go to *Teams apps* > *Manage apps*.
. Search for the MuleSoft app by name and select it.
+
The app details page shows who submitted the app and a *Pending action* status.
+
NOTE: The *Pending approval* count on the *Manage apps* page can take time to update. Search for the app by name instead of relying on this count.
. Click *Publish*.
+
Microsoft Teams Admin Center confirms that the app is published. It can take a few hours for the app status to update.

After the app is published and connected, *Notifications* > *Settings* > *Teams* shows *Installed*, and the *MuleSoft for Teams* card shows *Connected to _<organization-name>_*.

Users can now add the app to Teams channels.

[[disconnect-teams]]
=== Disconnect the MuleSoft for Teams App

. Log in to https://omni.mulesoft.com/[enhanced MuleSoft experience].
. Go to *Notifications* > *Settings* > *Teams*.
. Click *Disconnect* in the *MuleSoft for Teams* card.
. Confirm the disconnection. This immediately revokes MuleSoft's access to your Teams tenant and stops all notifications.
+
The MuleSoft app remains in Microsoft Teams Admin Center. To fully remove it, a Teams admin must uninstall it from the Admin Center, or right-click the app in Teams and select *Uninstall*.

[[microsoft-graph-permissions]]
== Microsoft Graph Permissions

The MuleSoft for Teams app requires the following Microsoft Graph permissions. A Teams admin grants these permissions once, on behalf of the organization, during <<grant-admin-consent>>. Use this information when your security or IT team asks for justification before approving the app installation.

[cols="2,1,3", options="header"]
|===
|Permission |Type |Purpose

|`AppCatalog.Submit`
|Delegated
|Submits the MuleSoft app package to your organization's app catalog for review, and cancels pending submissions. A Teams admin must approve and publish the submitted app in Microsoft Teams Admin Center before users can add it.

|`offline_access`
|Delegated
|Allows the app to maintain its connection without requiring users to reauthenticate every time they open Teams. The app stores a refresh token to request new short-lived access tokens. This permission doesn't grant any data access beyond what was already authorized.

|`AppCatalog.Read.All`
|Application
|Verifies that the MuleSoft app is properly published in your Teams app catalog. Without this, the app cannot confirm its own availability, and users see silent failures instead of actionable setup errors.

|`AppCatalog.ReadWrite.All`
|Application
|Allows the MuleSoft app to receive updates, such as new Adaptive Card formats or capability additions, without requiring an admin to manually republish each time.

|`Channel.ReadBasic.All`
|Application
|Delivers API alerts and deployment notifications to specific channels you configure. The app reads channel names and IDs to resolve routing rules. It reads channel metadata only — not message content.

|`Team.ReadBasic.All`
|Application
|During setup, lets you map MuleSoft environments and business groups to specific teams without manually looking up team IDs. Read-only access to team names and descriptions — no access to members or content.

|`TeamsAppInstallation.ReadWriteAndConsentForTeam.All`
|Application
|Lets a Teams admin roll the app out to all relevant teams in a single action with automatic RSC consent, rather than requiring each individual team owner to install it separately.

|`TeamsAppInstallation.ReadWriteForTeam.All`
|Application
|Manages the app lifecycle across teams, including handling version upgrades and removing the app from teams where it is no longer needed.

|`User.Read`
|Delegated
|Standard sign-in permission. The app reads basic profile information, such as name, email, and company, to map the Teams user to the correct Anypoint Platform identity and enforce role-based access control. It doesn't access contacts, calendars, or other profile data.

|===

[[security-data-compliance]]
== Security, Data Retention, and Residency

* *Hyperforce and data boundaries*: The MuleSoft Agent respects your designated Hyperforce region. Install the regional app for Teams that corresponds to your region. Customer data never leaves your designated Hyperforce boundary.
* *LLM architecture and web access*: The MuleSoft Agent uses the Salesforce LLM-Gateway, powered by OpenAI GPT-5-mini. The agent does not perform public web searches.
* *Data retention and compliance*: Data retention policies, data removal, LLM data tenancy, and infrastructure details are covered in the applicable Salesforce Security, Privacy, and Architecture (SPARC) documentation. Customer data is securely deleted upon expiration of the applicable retention periods in accordance with SPARC guidelines. For more information, see the https://www.salesforce.com/company/legal/trust-and-compliance-documentation/[Salesforce Trust and Compliance Documentation].

[[begin-using-agent]]
== Use the MuleSoft Agent

_If your Anypoint Platform organization is part of the early access program_, you can use natural language to interact with MuleSoft data from Teams:

* *Find services by capability*: Use natural-language search to discover APIs, agents, LLMs, and MCP servers.
* *Run governance, monitoring, and cost drill-ins*: Generate governance, monitoring, and cost reports on request.
* *Take governance actions*: Apply policies and apply cost management recommendations.

[IMPORTANT]
====
MuleSoft Agent isn't available to additional customers at this time. 
====

Before getting started, make sure you have:

* An Anypoint Platform account with access to the enhanced experience.

* Generative AI enabled in your Anypoint Platform and Salesforce organizations by your admin. For more information, see xref:access-management::enabling-generative-ai.adoc[].
* The *MuleSoft Agent AI User* permission assigned to your user.
+
For more information, see xref:exp-home-start.adoc#permissions[Enhanced Experience Permissions].

To assign the *MuleSoft Agent AI User* permission across business groups:

. Log in to Anypoint Platform and open *Access Management*.
. Select the business group where you want to assign the permission.
. Find the user and assign the *MuleSoft Agent AI User* permission.
. Repeat for each business group that requires access.

To start using the agent in Teams:

. Open Microsoft Teams and go to *Apps*.
. Under *Built for your organization*, find *MuleSoft* and click *Add*.
. Click *Open* or select a channel where you want to use the app.
. The agent sends a welcome message. Type `signin`.
. In the *Connect your MuleSoft account to continue* card, click *Connect to MuleSoft* and sign in with your Anypoint Platform credentials. This is a one-time setup.
. When the *Connected to MuleSoft* page appears, close the browser tab. Return to Teams to continue the conversation.

After you authenticate, you can use the agent to list APIs, agents, MCP servers, view governance reports, and more.

NOTE: In channels, @mention the MuleSoft Agent to send it a message. In direct messages, you can send messages to the agent at any time without @mentioning it.

[[available-prompts]]
=== Available Prompt Suggestions

When you open the MuleSoft Agent, the following prompt suggestions are available:

* *What business group am I in?*: Shows your current business group.
* *List APIs in my current business group*: Lists APIs in your current business group.
* *What risks exist across my portfolio?*: Shows risks across your portfolio.
* *Which MCP servers had the highest token spend this week?*: Shows which MCP servers had the highest token spend this week.
* *Which agents have instances?*: Lists agents that have instances.
* *What APIs have the highest error rates over the past 7 days?*: Shows APIs with the highest error rates over the past 7 days.

To send feedback to the MuleSoft Agent team, type `/feedback` in a channel or react to an agent response with a thumbs up or thumbs down.
